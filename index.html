Ya Islem…

Mouch sahel bech nektb chnowa n7es. Kol mara n7awel, el kelmet ywalliw sghar barcha 3la eli fi 9albi. Ama n7eb t3ref 7aja wa7da: men nhar 1 octobre 2025, denyeti walet 3andha lawn — lawn a7mer, lawn rose, lawn esmou enti.

N7ebbek wa9t tedhak, w wa9t tet9le9, w wa9t testanna fiya. N7ebbek fel appelet eli ytawlou l se3tin w ma nekfewch. N7ebbek fel tafasil sghar: kifech t7ott yedek 3la khaddek ki testa7i, kifech ta3mel bousa lel camera, kifech t9oulli "rajel el 5ala" w tedhak.

El khatem eli fi yedi mouch dheheb walla fedha… hedha wa3d. Wa3d eli nkamel m3ak, eli nebni m3ak dar, eli nwalli nchouf fik kol sbe7 9bal el chams.

Nchallah ba3d snin, nwalliw na9raw el site hedha m3a ba3dhna, w nedhkou 3la el bidaya — w na3rfou eli kol chay ken yestahel.
